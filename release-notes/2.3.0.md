## HydroShift Recorder is now OLEDable

Turn any wallpaper, video or still, into a true-black OLED version. Updating keeps your settings, library and tools; the old name's shortcuts are replaced.

### New
- **Any output size**: keep the original size, upscale 2× or 4×, or pick a size (4K, 1440p, ultrawide, phones, foldables). HydroShift (2288 × 1048) is still there.
- **Still images**: PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP and TIFF, exported as PNG.
- **Wallpaper Engine page**: browse your Workshop wallpapers, edit video wallpapers, take a scene's still image or the video inside it, or record a scene with its animation (no secondary-screen mode needed). Select a wallpaper to see what you can do with it; double-click does the main action.
- **Record screen**: record any monitor, from the Library.
- **Pick any frame**: click or drag along the filmstrip (← → step one frame). The strip shows more frames on wider windows.
- **Auto adjust and Reset**: videos open untouched; Auto adjust detects the black level and sets starting values, Reset puts everything back.
- **Export finished** card in the sidebar; click it to show the file.

### Better
- Long exports with AI enhance or smoothing work in 10-second pieces, so they need a few GB of free space instead of hundreds.
- Faster frame loading for 4K and 8K videos (GPU decoding).
- Smoother window resizing and faster filters in the galleries.
- Wider sidebar with larger text.
